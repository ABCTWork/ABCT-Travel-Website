Nepal

Nepal | 8 Days / 7 Nights

A Sacred Himalayan Road Journey

Through Heritage, Mountains & Muktinath
“Where every road has a story, every mountain carries a silence, and every temple offers a moment to pause.”

There are journeys that take you from one destination to another — and then there are journeys that take you deeper into a land, its culture, its spirituality and its mountains.

From the ancient temples and heritage squares of Kathmandu to the tranquil waters of Phewa Lake, from the rugged Himalayan roads of Kali Gandaki to the sacred shrine of Muktinath, and from the traditional Gurung village of Ghandruk to the magnificent Annapurna landscapes — this is Nepal experienced on the road, at your own pace.

🛤️ Getting There 🛤️

Arrival Point: Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu.

Route: Kathmandu (3N) → Pokhara (2N) → Jomsom (1N) → Ghandruk (1N) → Kathmandu

Transportation: Private 4×4 road trip – Kathmandu to Kathmandu

Meal Plan: CP / MAP as selected

Our representative will receive you at the airport and assist with your transfer to the hotel. The entire road journey is planned on a private vehicle basis, allowing greater flexibility and comfort throughout the trip.

🌄 Why Choose This Nepal Journey ?
• Heritage Meets Himalayas – some of Nepal's most important cultural, spiritual and natural experiences in one carefully designed road journey
• Sacred Nepal – Pashupatinath, Manakamana, Muktinath and Doleshwar Mahadev
• Himalayan Road Adventure – spectacular mountain roads towards Jomsom and Muktinath in a private 4×4 vehicle
• Culture Beyond the Cities – traditional Gurung lifestyle, stone-paved lanes and mountain atmosphere of Ghandruk
• A Journey, Not Just a Package – time for temples, landscapes, local culture, mountain roads and unexpected moments

📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival in Kathmandu

• Hotel Check-in • Pashupatinath Darshan • Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu, our representative will welcome you and transfer you to your hotel by private vehicle.
• After check-in and some time to relax, proceed for Pashupatinath Temple Darshan, subject to arrival time.
• If time permits, experience the spiritual atmosphere of the evening Bagmati Aarti beside the sacred Bagmati River.
• Overnight Stay: Kathmandu
• Meals: As per selected plan


2️⃣ Day 2 – Kathmandu Heritage & Cultural Exploration

• After breakfast, proceed for a full-day exploration of Kathmandu Valley.
Sightseeing includes:
• Swayambhunath Stupa – also known as the Monkey Temple, offering panoramic views over Kathmandu Valley
• Kathmandu Durbar Square – a historic royal palace complex surrounded by temples, courtyards and traditional architecture
• Boudhanath Stupa – one of the world's largest Buddhist stupas and an important centre of Tibetan Buddhist culture
• Patan Durbar Square – famous for its traditional Newari architecture, ancient palace courtyards and temples
• Bhaktapur Durbar Square – a beautifully preserved medieval city featuring temples, traditional architecture, Nyatapola Temple and local pottery areas
• Sanga – known for its monumental Shiva statue and impressive Himalayan and valley views
• Doleshwar Mahadev Temple – an important Shiva temple near Bhaktapur
• Sundarijal – optional, time permitting
• Due to the number of attractions, the sightseeing sequence may be adjusted according to traffic, opening hours and available time.
• Overnight Stay: Kathmandu
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


3️⃣ Day 3 – Kathmandu → Manakamana → Pokhara

• After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic road journey towards Pokhara.
• En route, visit the sacred Manakamana Temple, one of Nepal's most revered pilgrimage destinations.
• Guests may take the famous Manakamana Cable Car to reach the temple, subject to operation and ticket availability.
• After Darshan, continue towards Pokhara through beautiful Himalayan foothills, rivers, forests and countryside.
• Upon arrival, check in at your hotel and relax. If time permits, enjoy some leisure time around Pokhara Lakeside.
• Overnight Stay: Pokhara
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


4️⃣ Day 4 – Pokhara Sightseeing → Jomsom

• After breakfast, explore selected attractions of Pokhara before beginning the spectacular mountain road journey towards Jomsom.
• Pokhara Sightseeing: Phewa Lake, Tal Barahi Temple, Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple, Seti Gorge, International Mountain Museum (time permitting)
• After sightseeing, continue towards Jomsom by private 4×4 vehicle.
• The route gradually transforms from the greener landscapes around Pokhara into the rugged Himalayan terrain of the Kali Gandaki region, offering dramatic mountain scenery along the way.
• Arrive in Jomsom, check in and relax.
• Overnight Stay: Jomsom
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


5️⃣ Day 5 – Jomsom → Muktinath Darshan → Ghandruk

• An early start today for one of the most spiritually significant experiences of the journey.
• Muktinath Darshan: visit the sacred Muktinath Temple, renowned for its spiritual significance, the traditional 108 water spouts and the eternal flame.
• After Darshan, return towards Jomsom and continue the spectacular mountain drive towards Ghandruk.
• The journey passes through dramatic Himalayan landscapes with magnificent views of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges, traditional settlements and mountain valleys.
• Arrive at Ghandruk, a beautiful traditional Gurung village. Spend the evening exploring the village and enjoying the peaceful mountain atmosphere.
• Overnight Stay: Ghandruk
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


6️⃣ Day 6 – Ghandruk Village Experience → Pokhara

• Wake up to the peaceful atmosphere of the Himalayas. After breakfast, explore the traditional village of Ghandruk.
• Ghandruk Highlights: traditional Gurung village, Gurung culture & local lifestyle, mountain viewpoints, Annapurna & Machhapuchhre views (subject to weather), Ghandruk village walk, local monastery / temples, traditional stone-paved streets, traditional mountain houses
• After exploration, drive back towards Pokhara. Upon arrival, check in at the hotel.
• The evening is free for Lakeside shopping, leisure and relaxation.

• Overnight Stay: Pokhara
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


7️⃣ Day 7 – Pokhara → Kathmandu

• After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic return journey to Kathmandu.
• Enjoy the changing landscapes of Nepal along the way — hill roads, rivers, forests, villages and countryside.
• Upon arrival in Kathmandu, check in at the hotel.
• Evening at leisure. Depending on time and interest, guests may enjoy Thamel shopping, a local market visit, a Nepali cultural experience or a Kathmandu evening walk.
• Overnight Stay: Kathmandu
• Meals: Breakfast / Dinner as per selected plan


8️⃣ Day 8 – Kathmandu → Airport Departure

• After breakfast, check out from the hotel.
• Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some free time for shopping, relaxation or a final taste of Kathmandu.
• Our representative will transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your onward journey.
• Tour ends with beautiful memories of Nepal!

📝 Important Travel Notes

  • Mountain Road Conditions: The Jomsom–Muktinath sector is a high-altitude mountain road. Road conditions may vary depending on weather and seasonal conditions.
  • Weather: Mountain weather can change rapidly. Sightseeing and road journeys may occasionally need to be adjusted for safety and operational reasons.
  • Sightseeing Sequence: The sequence of sightseeing may be modified depending on traffic, local conditions, opening hours, weather and available time while maintaining the major attractions wherever possible.
  • Muktinath Visit: Early departure is recommended for a comfortable Muktinath Darshan and to make the most of the mountain journey.
  • 4×4 Journey: A private 4×4 is used for the rugged Himalayan road sectors to provide greater suitability for the terrain.

✅ Do’s

  • Carry a valid passport and required travel documents
  • Keep essential medicines and personal belongings with you
  • Carry warm clothing for Jomsom, Muktinath and Ghandruk
  • Follow local temple customs and dress respectfully
  • Keep adequate water with you during mountain journeys
  • Follow the instructions of the driver and local representatives
  • Respect local communities, traditions and religious places
  • Keep the mountains clean and avoid unnecessary plastic waste

🚫 Don’ts

  • Do not litter in the mountains or around religious sites
  • Do not disturb local customs, rituals or prayer areas
  • Avoid carrying unnecessary valuables
  • Do not expect fixed travel times on mountain roads where conditions can change
  • Do not undertake optional activities without checking operational and weather conditions
