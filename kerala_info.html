Kerala Journey

Kerala

"A Journey Through Backwaters, Hills, Wildlife & the Arabian Sea"

Kerala is not just a destination—it is a rhythm. From the historic lanes of Kochi to the misty tea gardens of Munnar, from the forests of Thekkady to the quiet backwaters of Alleppey, and finally to the beaches of Kovalam, this journey brings together many different faces of Kerala in one beautiful route.

Route: Kochi → Munnar → Thekkady → Alleppey → Kovalam → Trivandrum

🛤️ Getting There

The journey begins at Kochi Railway Station, making this itinerary convenient for travellers arriving by train. The tour concludes at Trivandrum Airport / Railway Station, creating a comfortable one-way travel route without unnecessary backtracking.

🌿 Why Choose This Kerala Journey?
This itinerary is designed to experience Kerala beyond a single postcard view.
• 🏛️ Heritage & Culture — Explore historic Kochi and its colonial-era charm
• 🌿 Misty Hills — Experience Munnar's tea gardens, waterfalls and mountain landscapes
• 🐘 Wildlife & Spice — Discover the forests and spice plantations of Thekkady
• 🚤 Backwater Life — Spend a night on a traditional Kerala houseboat at Alleppey
• 🌊 Coastal Kerala — End your journey with Kovalam, Poovar and the Arabian Sea
• 🛣️ A Complete Kerala Route — Hills, forests, backwaters, culture and beaches in one journey

📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival in Kochi

Kochi Railway Station → Hotel
Your Kerala journey begins with a pickup from Kochi Railway Station. Transfer to the hotel and complete your check-in. After some rest, you may explore the local market depending on your arrival time.
• Overnight Stay: Kochi

2️⃣ Day 2 – Kochi Local Sightseeing

Discover the Heritage & Urban Charm of Kochi
After breakfast, explore some of Kochi's most interesting landmarks.
Sightseeing:
• Fort Kochi
• Chinese Fishing Nets
• Jewish Synagogue
• Marine Drive
• Lulu Mall & Shopping
The day brings together Kochi's historic character and its modern urban side.
• Overnight Stay: Kochi

3️⃣ Day 3 – Kochi → Munnar

Waterfalls, Tea Gardens & Mountain Roads
After breakfast, begin your scenic journey towards Munnar, travelling through the beautiful landscapes of Kerala's Western Ghats.
En-route Experiences:
• Cheeyappara Waterfalls
• Valara Waterfalls
• Tea Gardens
• Spice Plantation
• Scenic Valley View Points
As the landscape gradually changes from the city to lush green hills, arrive in Munnar and check in to your hotel.
• Overnight Stay: Munnar

4️⃣ Day 4 – Munnar Local Sightseeing

Lakes, Tea & the High Ranges
Today is dedicated to exploring the scenic surroundings of Munnar.
Sightseeing:
• Mattupetty Dam
• Echo Point
• Kundala Lake
• Flower Garden
• Eravikulam National Park
• Tea Museum
Spend the day surrounded by tea-covered hills, mountain air and the distinctive landscape of the Western Ghats.
• Overnight Stay: Munnar
Note: Entry to attractions such as Eravikulam National Park may be subject to operational conditions and availability.

5️⃣ Day 5 – Munnar → Thekkady

Into the Forests & Spice Country
After breakfast, depart for Thekkady, one of Kerala's most fascinating wildlife and spice-growing regions.
Experiences:
• Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
• Periyar Lake Boating
• Spice Plantation Tour
• Kathakali Show
• Kalaripayattu Show
The evening offers an opportunity to experience two distinctive traditions of Kerala—its classical performance culture and the ancient martial art of Kalaripayattu.
• Overnight Stay: Thekkady
Note: Wildlife activities and boating are subject to availability and prevailing forest regulations.

6️⃣ Day 6 – Thekkady → Alleppey

The Backwater Experience
After breakfast, drive towards Alleppey, Kerala's celebrated backwater destination.
Upon arrival, board your Deluxe Houseboat and begin a relaxing cruise through Kerala's waterways.
Experiences:
• Houseboat Check-in
• Alleppey Backwater Cruise
• Village Life
• Paddy Field Views
• Scenic Backwater Landscapes
Slow down, watch village life unfold along the waterways and spend the night surrounded by Kerala's tranquil backwaters.
• Overnight Stay: Deluxe Houseboat
• Meals on Houseboat: All Meals (APAI)

7️⃣ Day 7 – Alleppey → Kovalam

Via Jatayu Earth Center
After breakfast, disembark from the houseboat and proceed towards Kovalam.
En-route Experiences:
• Jatayu Earth Center & Adventure Park
• Scenic En-route Views
• Beach Views
• Kovalam Beach
Arrive at Kovalam and spend the evening beside the Arabian Sea.
• Overnight Stay: Kovalam

8️⃣ Day 8 – Kovalam & Poovar

Beaches, Backwaters & Golden Sands
Today is dedicated to the coastal beauty of southern Kerala.
Sightseeing:
• Lighthouse Beach
• Hawa Beach
• Poovar Island
• Poovar Backwater Boating
• Golden Sand Beach
Experience the unique meeting of backwaters, mangroves and the Arabian Sea around Poovar before returning to Kovalam.
• Overnight Stay: Kovalam

9️⃣ Day 9 – Kovalam → Trivandrum

The Journey Comes to an End
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Trivandrum.
Sightseeing:
• Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple
• Napier Museum
• Trivandrum Zoo
Later, transfer to Trivandrum Airport / Railway Station for your onward journey.
Tour Ends — but the memories of Kerala travel with you.
Temple bells, tea-covered mountains, quiet backwaters and the sound of the Arabian Sea—Kerala leaves behind more than photographs. It leaves behind a rhythm.

✅ Do's

  • Carry reusable water bottles whenever possible
  • Respect local communities, traditions and wildlife
  • Follow instructions at wildlife sanctuaries and protected areas
  • Keep the backwaters, beaches and hills clean
  • Travel slowly and enjoy the destination

🚫 Don'ts

  • Do not litter in forests, beaches or backwaters
  • Do not disturb wildlife
  • Avoid single-use plastic wherever possible
  • Do not damage coral, plants or other natural surroundings
